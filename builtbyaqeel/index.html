What's inside

Five prompts. Production-ready.

01
Deal intake → structured brief

A mortgage brokerage was processing every new deal manually — reading through form submissions and pulling out key terms by hand. This prompt takes raw deal intake data and returns a clean, structured brief: loan amount, LTV, DSCR, property type, risk flags, and a plain-English deal summary. Runs in under 3 seconds. Replaced 20 minutes of manual work per deal.

20 min saved per deal
02
Lender / vendor matching with reasoning

Most matching logic is just filters. This prompt does filters plus reasoning — it scores candidate lenders against a deal profile, explains why each one does or doesn't fit, and ranks them with a confidence score. Built for a lending brokerage, but the pattern works for any situation where you're matching one record against a database of options.

4–5× match accuracy
03
Lead qualifier from form submission

An event production company was manually reading every contact form and deciding how to respond. This prompt reads the raw form data and outputs: lead temperature (hot/warm/cold), a one-line summary, recommended next action, and a suggested reply draft. Wired to a Supabase insert and Resend — the owner gets a qualified brief in their inbox within 60 seconds of a form submission.

60 sec qualified brief
04
CRM note → follow-up message writer

A realtor had months of notes on every lead but was writing follow-up messages from scratch every time. This prompt reads the full note history for a contact and writes a personalized follow-up that references specific details — the house they toured, their timeline, their hesitation. Feels human. Tested against a team of 3 realtors across 80+ leads without a single "this feels AI-generated" flag.

80+ leads tested
05
Pre-call client research brief

Before every sales call I run this prompt. Feed it a company name, LinkedIn URL, and whatever context you have — it outputs a structured brief: their likely pain points by industry, questions to open with, what a good fit looks like, and red flags. Takes 90 seconds. I walk into every call knowing more about their problem than they expect anyone to know before a first meeting.

90 sec call prep time
